About

I'm a Wildlife Conservation & GIS Specialist based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, with a background in Forestry Science from Universiti Putra Malaysia. My work sits at the intersection of field conservation and data analysis; turning patrol records, camera-trap data, and spatial datasets into insight that protects the Malayan Tiger. and other wildlife.

As Tiger Landscape Officer under the Global Wildlife Program, I work alongside the Department of Wildlife and National Parks (PERHILITAN) to process and validate spatial data using SMART (Spatial Monitoring and Reporting Tool) across the Southern Zone, including the Endau-Rompin complex; integrating datasets from PERHILITAN, State Parks, and partner NGOs into a unified monitoring system.

Before this, I managed daily operations across 1,340 sq km of the Al-Sultan Abdullah Royal Tiger Reserve for Pahang State Park Corporation, and worked as a Wildlife Researcher with the Wildlife Conservation Society (WCS) deploying camera traps and building patrol databases. I also design and deliver GIS/SMART training workshops for rangers and PERHILITAN staff.
